02 · Buy PT

Buy a principal token.

A principal token (PT) pays its full face value in USDC on the maturity date. You pay less than that today, and the difference is your fixed interest.

No maturities are registered on this deployment yet, so there is nothing to choose — this isn’t a bug, the date list is empty because the owner hasn’t registered a series. Check Rates for status.

Compatible wallets batch approval and purchase. Other wallets confirm two transactions.

03 · Buy YT

Buy a yield token.

A yield token (YT) collects all the variable interest the vault earns on its share of the deposit until the maturity date. It pays no principal back, so it gains if the vault earns more than you paid and loses if it earns less.

You deposit USDC, the fixed half is sold to the pool and that USDC comes straight back to you. What you keep is the yield token. How this works.

Connect your wallet first. Your wallet confirms an approval, the purchase, then an approval reset.

Your preview

Choose a maturity and amount to see what comes back and what the yield token costs.
