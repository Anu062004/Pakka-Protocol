02 · How it works

Three steps, no rate guessing.

One deposit splits into two tokens. Holding both equals the underlying position. The protocol never sets a rate — the pool price does.

Read the guide
01

Split

A vault position becomes a principal token (PT), worth exactly 1 USDC at maturity, and a yield token (YT) that collects every bit of variable interest until then.

02

Buy at a discount

Buy PT below 1 USDC on the open market. The discount is your fixed rate — known the moment you buy.

03

Redeem on the date

On maturity day, redeem PT for exactly 1 USDC. No rate math, no checking back — the ticket already told you the number.

03 · The people

A saver, an agent, and the market between them.

Rahul's agent · Budgets

Arjun · Yield side

Takes the other side.

He thinks Morpho's rate is going up, so he buys YT. The two sides always add up to one asset — that's what makes the fixed rate possible.

04 · Built for agents

A safe your agent can't empty.

A Tijori — Hindi for safe — is a small contract the owner deploys. The agent acts inside it, never outside it. The contract enforces the limits, not the agent process.

The agent can

Buy PT for registered series onlyCash out matured PT, claim YT interestPay approved payees within per-payee and daily caps

The agent cannot

Send funds to any address it choosesChange its own limits, payees, or routerAct after the owner pauses it
